Our Mission & Legacy

Connecting the past, present, and future of agriculture leaders.

Empowering Through Connection

Founded in 1985, the Agri Alumni Association has been the bridge between the university and its graduates. Our mission is to foster a lifelong intellectual and emotional connection between the college and its alumni.

We believe in the power of community to drive innovation in agriculture, sustainability, and food security.
